## 1. Identify the finite image and justify its generating rank

Set Q=R^sigma/(K∩R^sigma), an R-module. It is a quotient of
R^sigma/cR^sigma, whose cardinal is C=(genIndex c)^r by BoxPartition
296–302, so 1≤q=|Q|≤C<∞. M_a⊆R^sigma, and its image W_a in Q is
canonically M_a/(M_a∩K). Therefore d_a=|W_a|≥1, not [Q:W_a].
This resolves the native nonzero-index guard without assuming n(a)>0.

The claim W_a is generated by r elements uses the SAME native
Hermite step as VolumeKernel 28–31, not an unproved free-module
claim over the adele ring. Put X=Y_a m, and apply Hermite to
Z=[X^T;0], of size (t+r)×r, with the inclusion of the last r rows.
It gives integral invertible L of size t+r and D of size r×r
such that Z=L_last D and D=(L^-1)_lastRows Z.
Taking the first t rows in the first identity and transposing both,
    X=D^T P^T,   D^T=X J^T,
where P=(L_last)_firstRows and J=((L^-1)_lastRows)_firstCols
are integral. Thus the two column modules of X and D^T are equal.
Its r columns generate M_a and hence W_a. No rank constancy,
nonzero determinant of D, division, or global free basis required.
This is precisely the padded source argument, including t=0 or r=0.

## 2. Intrinsic quotient partition and exact weight cancellation

under the prescribed equivalence (PartnerAlgebra 95–97).

of Y_a U. Let w_a∈Q^t be its first-column residue tuple; w_a∈W_a^t.

Partition B by the intrinsic invariant (W_a,v_a), not a choice of
generators modulo c. There are at most q^r possible W: every such
submodule is generated by an r-tuple of Q, so the map from Q^r

number of nonempty parts is ≤q^(r+s)≤C^(r+s).

generators, unlike the existing sufficient first colour modulo c.

Fix one part (W,v) with d=|W|. At most d^t possible w_a occur.

modulo K, hence all columns of Y_a congruent modulo K. H bounds

when l>0 and n(a)=0 this is zero, NOT ENNReal.top. For l=0 the

All finite partial sums in a part are therefore bounded by
    d^t * x * d^(-l) = x * d^(t-l) ≤ x.

not truncated natural subtraction. All d are finite positive.

x=top gives a trivial global bound since C≥1.
