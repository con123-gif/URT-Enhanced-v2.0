## 2. Vanishing for all positive even j at l=3 or 7

If l-1 divides j, TorsClass.zOne already supplies Chr.V=0. This covers l=3 and multiples of 6 at l=7.
Otherwise l=7 and j even, j not divisible by 3. The residual cyclotomic action on M[7] has kernel G_F, F=Q(zeta_7)^+, because the map a -> a^(-j) on F_7^* has kernel {±1}. F is real cubic with discriminant <=49: its generator zeta_7+zeta_7^-1 satisfies irreducible monic X^3+X^2-2X-1 (rational root test) with discriminant 49. Its Minkowski bound is <=(3! / 3^3)*sqrt(49)=14/9<2. Hence its ideal class group is trivial.
Take a class u in the source Selmer kernel killed by 7. The exact sequence 0->M[7]->M --7-> M ->0 implies u has a continuous 1-cocycle representative z with values in M[7] (equivalently subtract a coboundary after choosing a 7-division point of the original coboundary, since M divisible). Away from 7, cyclotomic inertia acts trivially on M, and local triviality implies z vanishes on inertia.
At any decomposition group over 7, z(g)=(alpha(g)-1)m for some m in M because the class is locally zero. The local cyclotomic character mod 7 is surjective (already inertia surjectivity), so some g has alpha(g)-1 a unit in Z_7 and O. Since z(g) is killed by 7, so is m. Therefore for every g in the intersection with G_F, alpha acts identically on M[7] and z(g)=0. The restriction z|G_F is a continuous additive homomorphism (action trivial) unramified at all finite places. Its finite image is 7-primary, so real-place inertia, of order at most 2, also maps to zero. By unramified class field theory it factors through Cl(F), which is trivial. Thus restriction is zero. Inflation-restriction now identifies the possible residual cocycle classes with H1(C3,M[7])=0 since 3 invertible on M[7]. Hence u=0.
Finally every continuous cocycle into the discrete 7-primary torsion M has finite image by compactness of the profinite absolute Galois group; the cohomology classes are all 7-power torsion. A nonzero such class would yield a nonzero class killed by 7. There are none, proving ShaU=0 and Chr.V=0. This argument generalizes the source zQuad residual-degree-2 mechanism to the explicit residual-degree-3 real field. It does not assert a general vanishing theorem for arbitrary l or twists.
