## 1. Replace all vector choices by one positive-type weight
At each chosen place w above q0, let π_w:G→GL_r(F_q) denote the actual residue representation. Define two n-dimensional subspaces U_w,0 and U_w,1 as the images of the full native frame matrices on the two sides of the leading minor (the frameImage of identity and splitGram respectively). Write π_w,0=π_w and π_w,1=π_w^{-T}; the source uses the ordinary and contragredient actions, respectively. Define
    c_w(s) = [ (q^a0(s)-1) + (q^a1(s)-1) ] / [2(q^n-1)],
where a_i(s)=dim(U_w,i ∩ ker(π_w,i(s)-I)).
This is the average of the two probabilities of fixing a uniformly selected nonzero vector in these subspaces. Hence 0<=c_w<=1 and c_w is positive type: for each nonzero vector z, the indicator of its stabilizer is the matrix coefficient of the permutation representation on its orbit (basis vector at z), and positive sums preserve positive type. No assumption that the subspaces are invariant under π_w is used. All nonzero vector fixers of every leading minor are covered, since their vectors belong to the full corresponding space. On each such fixer c_w(s)>=1/[2(q^n-1)], or more sharply (q-1)/[2(q^n-1)], because all nonzero multiples of the vector are fixed. Use the conservative constant L_q=2q^n so 1_fixer <= L_q c_w pointwise.

## 2. One deterministic chain, no vector-by-vector induction
Fix any order w1,...,wf of all chosen places above q0. Set W_0=1 and W_j=∏_{i<=j}c_wi. Let H_j for j<f be the set of s for which all leading minors at w_{j+1} are nonzero; H_f=G. Let E(s)=native full normalized amplitude energy over singleton {s}; nonnegative and additive over subsets. Put e=b^-4, C=mainEnergy, u=b^(-28k/5), K_j=(1+9e)C u^j.
The ONLY new arithmetic inputs for this replacement are the f+1 weighted scalar estimates
    ∑_{s∈H_j} E(s) W_j(s) <= (1-e) K_j,    j=0,...,f.
They do not assert bounds for individual vectors or any other subsets of places. The j=0 good set uses one predetermined place, a subset of the native open-cell union, so its arithmetic demand is no stronger. For j=f there is no open-cell condition, as in native fixingSet when all places are selected.

Let Θ≥0 be the original nonnegative positive-type function dominated pointwise by E(s). Set Θ_j=Θ W_j (positive type via tensor products of its Gram representation and those of c_w), M_j=Σ_s Θ_j(s). Reverse induction in integer j, starting at f: M_f<=Σ E W_f<=(1-e)K_f<=K_f. If M_j>K_j for j<f, the good-set weighted energy bound gives Σ_{s∉H_j} Θ_j(s) > e M_j. For any nonzero vector/covector in a leading frame at w_{j+1}, its fixer mass under Θ_j is
    Σ Θ_j 1_fixer <= L_q Σ Θ_j c_w = L_q M_{j+1}
    <= L_q K_{j+1} < L_q u M_j.
Apply the unchanged native card_mul_sum_det_le to Θ_j and each of the n leading minors with this Y. The field/rank exponent obeys

This is a new sufficient hypothesis for the actual terminal positive-type moment caller; it does not reproduce the original conditioned conclusions for arbitrary A,D. The proof uses the original uniform finite Fourier lemma unchanged, avoiding a new Fourier proof. The large rank slack absorbs the cost of averaging over all vectors in the full frame. The chain length is f+1 instead of 2^f possible subset levels, and each level uses one explicit weight rather than a family of per-vector energy estimates. The original theorem is a single uniform proof (not 2^f separate proofs); this is a reduction of the sufficient arithmetic target, not a literal claim about Lean proof size/runtime.

## 3. Connection to existing estimates and possible arithmetic bypass
For each j, W_j is the average of the products of the relevant individual vector-fixer indicators over independent uniform selections of side and nonzero full-frame vector at the first j places. Thus the original fixingSet energy estimates imply these weighted ones by averaging: for j<f, H_j implies the native condition of some place with all leading minors nonzero; for j=f the original set has no open-cell guard. Using the earlier relaxed bound with exponent 28k/5 also suffices. This comparison retains all original arithmetic guards and does not import the original unconditioned moment into the new proof.

The weighted premises are genuinely aggregate conditions: each term can be written in terms of fixed-space dimensions (kernel ranks of two r by n matrices per place), no enumeration of q^n nonzero vectors required for evaluating weights. A proof of the weighted estimates via lattice count of such ranks, finite-field permutation character traces, or directly averaged local orbit/volume kernels would not need uniform bounds for each individual stabilizer. The displayed formula alone does not bound E(s); one cannot replace E(s) by a uniform distribution. The identity element is a useful negative control (all c_w=1, all leading minors may fail; the terminal estimate is indispensable).

The native floorZeroForm is an integral over the entire local group, not just its finite congruence quotient. Restricting to integral matrices would omit noncompact exterior contributions, so a finite-field orbit count by itself does not prove the needed weighted energy. TopFloor.lean (blob 9762970967aef90b3a4e025b7f2891d892c70111) uses lintegral_shells_le for floorZeroForm_le_main_term AND enorm_floorForm_le. Those feed the unconditioned central-trace and sieve frequency guards. Replacing the conditioned bottom bound alone would not remove the shell theorem globally.
