## 3. New clipped source-leverage certificate (proved finite-dimensional lemma)
Let d'=dim W', M positive definite on W', Z_s=M^-1/2 rho(s) T. Then E_s Z_s Z_s*=I_W', Z_s*Z_s=L for every s, tr L=d'. Set a=4d/v, sigma=b^-3/2. It suffices to establish only
    d-d' <= sigma*d/4,
    Tr[(L-aI)_+] <= sigma*d/4.
Unlike the preceding ||L||≤a criterion, this allows individual eigenvalues arbitrarily above a up to the intrinsic bound |G|, provided their integrated excess is small.

Proof: choose the common positive invertible coordinate map D on X with eigenvalue sqrt(min(1,a/lambda)) on each L-eigenspace with lambda>0, and eigenvalue 1 on its kernel. Let Y_s=Z_s D, and C=E_s Y_sY_s*. Then 0≤C≤I_W', ||Y_s||²≤a, and
    tr(I_W'−C)=tr(L−min(L,aI))=Tr[(L−aI)_+]=E.
All ranges of Y_s are the whitened original ranges because D is invertible and M^-1/2 is a COMMON invertible target map on W'. For missing-space projector J of rank r in W', tr(J C)≥r−E. Some s has rank(J Y_s)≥||JY_s||_HS²/a≥(r−E)/a. If a<1, use a0=max(1,a); in fact the rank/trace guards yield v≤4d for the intended application using native near-injectivity, but this is not a consequence of the two new premises alone. Retain v≤4d as a separate guard or derive it from a weaker source-rank estimate. With a≥1, greedy selection while r>E+sigma*d/4 gives recurrence r_next−E ≤ (1−1/a)(r−E). Thus j=ceil[a log(4/sigma)] steps suffice, r≤E+sigma*d/4≤sigma*d/2. The cost jv≤4d log(4/sigma)+v≤5d log(1+b²), for b≥1000 and v≤4d: LHS/d≤6log b+4log4+4≤10log b. Positive gain ensures no repeated translate. In W the total full-map deficit is at most (d−d')+r≤3sigma*d/4. This is exactly the relaxed rank/translate interface in 1879ed19189a3cdd78762e13f46a079f9d91a2d6, with unchanged A/C and unrounded compact height argument. ## 4. Turn actual source symmetry into a probability target
Decompose the unitary H-representation X=⊕_tau V_tau⊗N_tau, h_tau=dim V_tau. By the verified algebraic consequence of the covariance, L=⊕ I_{V_tau}⊗L_tau, L_tau≥0 on multiplicity space N_tau. The exact clipped excess becomes
    E=Σ_tau h_tau tr[(L_tau−aI)_+].
Thus the scalar probability measure on the eigenvalues of L, normalized by d', is the ACTUAL source leverage distribution. The finite reference Gelfand-Graev label weights do not compute these multiplicity matrices. A sufficient trace-only condition is
    Σ_tau (tr(L|X_tau)−a h_tau)_+≤sigma*d/4,
because for any nonnegative sequence x_i, Σ_i(x_i−a)_+≤(Σ_i x_i−a)_+. If N_tau is one-dimensional the trace-only inequality is exact. In general this bound may be very wasteful; no assertion that it holds for native source. The useful new target is to bound the aggregate excess, not prove that every source eigenvalue is uniformly controlled or that all source directions are irreducibly symmetric.
