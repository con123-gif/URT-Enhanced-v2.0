## 1. Exact weight comparison and dimension-independent total variation
Let G=GL_r(F_q), c a squarefree monic degree-r polynomial with nonzero constant coefficient, irreducible factor degrees d_i summing to r. Write P=∏_{j=1}^r(1-q^-j), D_c=∏_i(1-q^-d_i). By the screenshot's degree formula for the regular irreducible in this squarefree series, its degree is |G|_{p'}/∏_i(q^d_i-1), where p is the characteristic. The source's labelChar for squarefree c is the irreducible obtained by parabolic induction of the distinct cuspidal factors (Lib/LocalGL/Green/FrobeniusOrbits.lean, Lib/LocalGL/Shintani/LabelCharacters.lean); identify the labels through the chosen faithful character. In the usual common Green labeling it is this regular irreducible. Then
    w_GG(c)=q^-r/D_c,
    w_reg(c)=dim(π_c)^2/|G|=q^-r P/D_c²,
    w_reg(c)/w_GG(c)=P/D_c.
The second is the actual regular coefficient of native labelIdem. By product Bernoulli, P≥1−Σ_{j=1}^r q^-j≥1−1/(q−1), and D_c≤1. Both distributions give each squarefree label at least q^-r P. There are at least q^r(1−2/q) such labels for r≥2: number of monic squarefree degree r is q^r−q^(r−1) (by the squarefree Euler product (1−qu²)/(1−qu)); at most q^(r−1) monic polynomials have zero constant. Thus on the common full set of irreducibles, with GG and regular normalized probability measures,
    TV(w_reg,w_GG) = 1−Σ_π min(w_reg(π),w_GG(π))
                  ≤ 1−(1−2/q)P ≤ 2/q+1/(q−1).
For q>2 this is dimension-independent O(1/q). Tensoring over f chosen places gives TV≤f(2/q+1/(q−1)) by telescoping and triangle inequality (or coupling). This allows comparison of any finite label event in these two *reference* distributions, including goodTuples. It is not an estimate of weights for the actual automorphic representation. Native BadShare.lean already has a sufficient estimate; this may be an alternative presentation but not a new B3 closure. In particular do not replace the native label-degree proof by a citation to a screenshot without checking the original literature and label convention.

## 2. Exact native covariance: a partial unipotent induction, not full GG
Source [AmplitudeSums.lean](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean/blob/72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207/grh-compact/src/New/Filling/AmplitudeSums.lean), blob 832e25af346bbbbdabdbec205b0e7f833170e0a1, amplitudeSum_besselU_mul:
    A(ū s (ū')^-1)=ψ(u)^-1 ψ(u') A(s).
Use full amplitude and T=normalized Poincare map from Moments.lean (same scalar for all columns). Its unfolding matrix is T*ρ(s)T=A(s)/ν. Hence for h=reduction(u) with u in the native integral Bessel unipotent,
    T*ρ(h)T=ψ(u)^-1 T*T.
With inner product and adjoint in the same convention as source, the corresponding eigencharacter is χ(h)=ψ(u)^-1. If T≠0, the character descends to the reduction image: setting h=1 forces ψ(u)^-1=1 for every kernel element. More generally the covariance alone gives the following identity for each u even if T=0. For every column x,
    ||ρ(h)Tx−ψ(u)^-1 Tx||²=0,
by unitarity, |ψ(u)|=1 and the compression identity. Thus range T, and also the centrally projected ramified range, lies in the χ-eigenspace for H=the reduction image of integral Bessel U. On nonzero T, the corresponding group-algebra idempotent e_H=|H|^-1 Σ_h overline(χ(h))h acts identically on the image. Equivalently the map from column coordinates extends G-equivariantly from a direct sum of v copies of Ind_H^G χ to the orbit span: [g]⊗x ↦ρ(g)Tx (with the matching character convention). This is the exact finite covariance bridge from native unfolding. The source's Bessel U is block upper unitriangular with a large middle block, and simpleRootSum reads the frame e_1,...,e_ϱ,z. It is not the full maximal unipotent U in Proposition 14.34. No assertion of an extension to its generic character or equality of the two induced representations.

## 3. Why explicit label weights alone cannot close the spectral certificate
For W=⊕_c V_c⊗M_c with V_c irreducible and M_c multiplicity space, let P denote the orthogonal projector onto the actual projected ramified image. Twirling P yields on sector c
    I_{V_c}⊗[(1/d_c)Tr_{V_c}(E_c P E_c)].
The rank-weighted lower spectrum depends on the eigenvalues on M_c, not just their sum. Even supposing the stronger maximal-unipotent covariance of the screenshot, the GG multiplicity-one theorem gives a one-dimensional χ-space L_c in each squarefree V_c, but the actual ambient χ-space is L_c⊗M_c. Fix a normalized generator ξ_c of L_c. If P has range in ⊕_c L_c⊗M_c, define B_c=(ξ_c*⊗I)P(ξ_c⊗I) on M_c. Then exact twirl block is I⊗B_c/d_c. The target from the earlier sector note is a bound on the sum of d_c times the numbers of small eigenvalues of B_c/d_c. The reference formula for wt(e_U,c) does not determine B_c.
Counterexample: take one squarefree irreducible V with a generic line L, and target W=V⊗C². Let the seed be L⊗span(e_1). It obeys full generic covariance and has all its label mass in the same sector as the full target, but its G-orbit never reaches V⊗e_2; the twirl has half its eigenvalues zero. This shows even exact label support and full GG multiplicity-one cannot establish the required rank coverage. One can equally use the same seed in each of many sectors to match a prescribed label trace distribution while leaving missing multiplicity directions.

A possible direct proof target is the quantitative surjectivity of the ramified Poincare coefficient map into the relevant Bessel multiplicity spaces, or an averaged lower bound on their partial traces. It must use arithmetic data, e.g. actual Bessel periods on the automorphic multiplicity spaces, not just character theory. Hecke trace transfer in GoodFormTransfer.lean gives dimension and Hecke trace equality between inner forms but does not establish the Poincare map's image or singular values. The separation theorem in Rows/Separation.lean is for different product theta maps, not for the Bessel map in FillingTheorem.lean. An intertwiner identifying those maps would be a substantive new lemma; do not assume one.
