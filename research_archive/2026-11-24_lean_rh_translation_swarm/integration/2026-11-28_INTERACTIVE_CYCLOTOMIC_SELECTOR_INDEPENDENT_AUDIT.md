## 1. Human audit — univariate supplier
The proposed direct shell proof is sound at the human mathematical level under the same standard p-adic algebraic-closure and valuation facts used in RUN96. Details checked independently:

* Normalize v(p)=1, radius r=|p|^t, thresholds y_k=|p|^b_k. The Gauss premise gives G_t(F_k)<=b_k; integral coefficients give G_0>=0. A nonzero polynomial of degree zero is allowed. If y_k>1 there are no admissible F_k; the empty family is immediate. Multiplicativity for a real radius follows by using the largest maximizing coefficient index in each factor: at their sum every competing term has an index strictly beyond a largest maximizer, hence strictly smaller norm. Thus factorization in the p-adic algebraic closure works even when r is not in the value group, and the budget sum over all roots of min(t,max(0,v(alpha))) is <=sum b_k, with v(0)=infinity interpreted by the cap. Roots outside the unit disk contribute zero.
* On primitive p^j shells each root x has valuation t_j=1/[p^(j-1)(p-1)]. For a root alpha on the same shell, choose x0 maximizing v(x-alpha). For x!=x0, v(x-alpha)<=v(x-x0): strict violation forces v(x0-alpha)=v(x-x0), contradicting maximality. If alpha=x0 the infinite term is capped at t, so it remains valid. Differentiating the factorization of X^(p^j)-1 at the corresponding root of unity gives sum_(x!=x0) v(x-x0)=j-1/(p-1). Subtracting (n_j-1)t_j=1-t_j gives j-1-1/(p-1)+t_j<=j-1. Therefore the sum of capped excesses per root is at most t+j-1. Other-valuation roots contribute exactly zero excess by the ultrametric equality for unequal valuations. This includes p=2,j=1, multiplicities and alpha=0.
* If every point of shell j fails some inequality, G_tj<=G_t<=b_k (since t_j<=t and coefficient valuation increases with radius exponent). Consequently that point must have total excess >t for a failed k. Capping each nonnegative excess at t leaves total >=t, including exact coincidences. Summing over all k and all points yields n_j t <=(t+j-1) C_j. The root budgets are on disjoint shells, so sum_j t_j C_j<=B. Universal failure through level N implies B>=sum_(j=j0)^N t/(t+j-1). This diverges, uniformly in degrees, ramification and choices of polynomials.
* Quantifier check: j0 depends only on p,r; N0 depends only on p,r,the finite fixed thresholds. For each N>=N0 the eventual coefficient-ring condition need only provide a primitive p^N root in that ring, because all smaller shells are powers of it. No assumption that the arbitrary polynomial roots belong to the coefficient ring is needed: factorization occurs in the algebraic closure, while the selected evaluation point lies in the given ring. This matches the two nested eventual quantifiers and the universal family quantifier in exists_root_of_unity_eval_large. The source's stronger radius premise is unused by this argument, but can be kept verbatim in a statement-preserving adapter.

For a simple explicit sufficient choice, sum_(j=j0)^N t/(t+j-1) >= t log((t+N)/(t+j0-1)) by decreasing-integrand comparison. Any integer N with t+N>(t+j0-1) exp(B/t) forces contradiction. This is not needed for the source caller, but confirms there is no hidden degree-dependent cutoff. It is a sufficient bound, not an efficient search estimate.
