## Native LemmaM applied to a common ideal rather than one trace
Fix SAME complete characteristic-zero coefficient DVR O, finite residue field of size q, uniformizer pi, algebraically closed valued extension K and valuation v normalized by v(pi)=1. Retain native M1–M4, IsWRing, and the same equation system Q, augmentation x, grading and valuation. Suppose a_1,...,a_k are degree-zero elements killed by x, and for every two-signed on-scheme point xi at least one xi(a_j) is nonzero. Suppose a finite native Covers representative set T is available (e.g. from original LemmaM with its original M5 witness; do not assume finiteness without a supplier). Put m_xi=min_j v(xi(a_j)), treating v(0)=+infinity, and N=|T|.

Then there exist coefficients c_j in O such that the SINGLE element a=sum_j c_j a_j meets M5, and
    sum_{xi in T} v(xi(a)) <= sum_{xi in T} m_xi + N/(q-1).
If q>N the additive allowance can be removed entirely.

Proof: independent probability Haar coefficients on the compact additive group O. For a fixed xi choose j attaining the finite minimum m_xi and condition on all other coefficients. Divide by xi(a_j); the resulting expression is c_j+b in K with v(b)>=0. For any t>0, the set of c in O with v(c+b)>=t is empty or contained in one coset modulo pi^ceil(t), because differences of two solutions have integral valuation at least ceil(t). Hence its probability is <=q^-ceil(t); it is zero for the event c+b=0. The nonnegative excess valuation has expectation <=integral_0^infty q^-ceil(t) dt=1/(q-1), by the tail-integral formula. This holds conditionally and then unconditionally. Finite sum gives expected total excess <=N/(q-1); some choice attains at most the stated bound, with no xi(a)=0. In fact each cancellation event of strictly positive excess has probability <=1/q by the same coset argument for 0<t<=1. If q>N, their union has probability <=N/q<1, so choose a vector with every excess zero. The argument works for rational fractional extension valuations; only valuations of differences of O-coefficients must be integral. N=0: any degree-zero base-vanishing a qualifies vacuously and LemmaM gives zero. No extension of the model, alteration of grading, or residual derivation assumed.

Each degree-zero evaluation is constant along the native grading orbit, hence nonvanishing at T implies M5 at all two-signed points. Since the sum is still degree zero and killed by x, the actual LemmaM gives finite cotLen C and
    C <= 2 sum_xi m_xi + 2N/(q-1)
(or no additive term for q>N).
By RUN26, C=v_pi(I_r) for the saturated cotangent maximal-minor ideal. This is a bound on the same determinant ideal, not a new deformation ring or an arbitrary residual tangent space. No dependence on the number k of trace tests in the overhead. The integral ideal generated by the evaluated traces has valuation m_xi in the valuation ring of K (valuation-ring convention, not a claim that it is an O-ideal).
