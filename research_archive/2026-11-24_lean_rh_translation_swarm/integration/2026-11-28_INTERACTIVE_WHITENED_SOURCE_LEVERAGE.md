## 1. Exact finite-dimensional theorem
Let W=D.rows, d=dim W>0, X=D.Column, v=dim X>0, and let T:X→W be the actual good-projected, ramified Poincare map at s=1, using ramifyingOp as a precomposition from X. Normalize by any nonzero scalar. By centrality of e0 all maps at s are T_s=ρ(s)T with unitary ρ on W. This is exactly FillingTheorem.lean plus ShortAndEnough.lean's range identification (nonzero seesaw scalar); preserve ramification. Let W'=span_s range(T_s), d'=dim W', and M=E_s T_s T_s* on W'. M is positive definite there: ker M=intersection_s ker T_s*=W'^perp in W, since each quadratic form is a squared norm. M commutes with ρ. Define
    L=T* (M|W')^-1 T    on X.
Here the inverse is zero on W'^perp if using the whole W; no numerical lower bound on M is assumed. Let A=M^-1/2 on W' and define Z_s=A T_s, so E_s Z_s Z_s*=I_W', and Z_s=ρ(s) Z_1.
For every s, ||Z_s||_op²=||Z_1* Z_1||_op=||L||_op. For any span F' of selected transformed ranges, let J project onto its complement in W', with dimension r. Then
    E_s ||J Z_s||_HS² = tr J = r.
For each s, ||J Z_s||_HS²≤||L||_op rank(J Z_s), because all squared singular values are ≤||L||_op. Hence some translate gains rank at least r/||L||_op. Since A is a COMMON invertible linear map on W', the dimensions of all sums of transformed ranges equal those of the original ranges. The change A is used only in proving rank; do not use A on the arithmetic family, covolume, integral lattice or twists.

## 2. A sufficient source-only threshold for actual endgame
Let σ=b^-3/2. Suppose
    d'≥(1−σ/2)d,
    ||L||_op≤4d/v.
Then greedily choose new translates while missing rank within W' exceeds σ d/2. At each step r_next≤(1−v/(4d))r; positivity implies v/(4d)≤1 (indeed ||L||≥1 if W'≠0 because average Z_s Z_s*=I). Thus with j=ceil[(4d/v) log(2/σ)], missing rank in W' is ≤σ d/2 and total original rank ≥(1−σ)d. Cost j v≤4d log(2/σ)+v≤5d log(1+b²) for b≥1000, as in direct sector note 1879ed19189a3cdd78762e13f46a079f9d91a2d6. Its unrounded compact height endgame applies eventually on the same actual native data, retaining A/C and original row basis. No lower bound on eigenvalues of M or of average projectors is needed.

The trace identity tr L = d' follows directly from tr M^-1 E_s T_s T_s*=d' and group invariance. Thus a sufficient alternative source condition is that the largest eigenvalue of L is at most four times its mean d'/v (this is slightly stronger than 4d/v), along with near-full orbit rank. This is a leverage-balancing condition, not ordinary near-isometry of T. It is invariant under replacing T by C T with ANY invertible C commuting with ρ on W', because M transforms to C M C* and L is unchanged. This is the precise sense in which singular-value magnitude is irrelevant.

## 3. Probability and multiplicity interpretation
Define F:direct sum_{s∈G} X→W by F((x_s))=Σ_s T_s x_s. Then FF*=|G|M. The orthogonal projection K=F*(FF*)^+ F onto ker(F)^perp has every diagonal X-block equal to L/|G|. Indeed block s is T_s* (FF*)^+ T_s and M commutes with ρ. Thus tr K=d', and L/|G| is an actual block of a projection on a finite source space, not a reference label distribution. In particular 0≤L≤|G|I, and its eigenvalues normalized by d' form a probability distribution. The desired source bound is a min-entropy type upper bound on that distribution. No random independence of automorphic labels is assumed.

If a group of source unitaries U acts irreducibly on X and for each U there is a target unitary V commuting with ρ with T U=V T, then U* L U=L (M commutes with V), hence L=(d'/v)I by Schur. More generally if X splits into inequivalent invariant source blocks with multiplicity one, L is scalar on each, and one needs only their trace weights divided by block dimensions. If X has multiplicities, the same obstruction recurs. These symmetry hypotheses are sufficient, not asserted for the actual columns.

## 4. Strict separation: why this is not the earlier spectral target in disguise
A strict separation is obtained by commuting invertible rescaling across representation sectors of a graph seed. Let G=C_2 act on W=C^m⊕C^m as (+I,−I). Let X=C^m, T x=(x,εx) with ε>0. Then the two translates span W, M=diag(I,ε² I), L=2I, d=2m, v=m, and ||L||=d/v. The average range projector equals diag(I/(1+ε²), ε² I/(1+ε²)). For ε sufficiently small its second block is below v/(4d)=1/8, so HALF the dimensions fail the earlier direct sector threshold. Yet two translates span every dimension exactly, independent of ε. This demonstrates genuinely different strength: the new criterion recognizes exact rank coverage even when numerical projection coverage is poor. No numerical example asserts an arithmetic result.
