## Unconditional fixed-field classical theorem
Fix any totally imaginary number field E (native CM host), prime l, and positive integer e. Write S_l for places above l and G_S for the maximal extension unramified outside S_l (complex places impose no extra condition). Let
    A_e = K_{2e}(O_E).
Quillen finite generation and Borel rank zero in positive even degrees imply A_e is finite. Quillen localization gives
    direct sum_{v|l} K_{2e}(k_v) -> K_{2e}(O_E)
      -> K_{2e}(O_E[1/l]) -> direct sum_{v|l} K_{2e-1}(k_v).
The first term is zero and the last has orders |k_v|^e-1, prime to l by Quillen finite-field computation. Tensor with flat Z_l; both endpoint terms vanish. Thus K_{2e}(O_E[1/l]) tensor Z_l = A_e tensor Z_l naturally. Quillen–Lichtenbaum for i=e+1>=2,j=2 (at odd l the established theorem, or just Soulé's Chern surjectivity for an upper bound; at l=2 Kahn's theorem 1 for totally imaginary fields) and the standard etale/G_S comparison for S_l containing all l-places give
    H^2(G_S,Z_l(e+1)) ~= A_e tensor Z_l.
For the compact-coefficient interpretation, finite-coefficient H^1 groups are finite and hence Mittag-Leffler, so the lim^1 term vanishes. Apply Poitou–Tate duality to Z_l(e+1) and Q_l/Z_l(-e). Their strict local kernels Sha^2 and Sha^1 are Pontryagin dual, with finite lengths equal, because the first is a subgroup of finite H^2. Therefore
    0 <= length_Z_l Sha^1(G_S,Q_l/Z_l(-e)) <= v_l(|A_e|).
This proves fixed-host finiteness for every l (including l=2), every e>0, independently of the native uniform window estimates. It is not a claim of an elementary proof of Quillen–Lichtenbaum.

For t finite define J(E,t)=product_{1<=e<t} |K_{2e}(O_E)|. Then the cohomological index I_D from 2d051914 divides J(E,t) at each prime, so globally
    I_D | J(E,t), and log I_D <= log J(E,t).
In particular the prime support is finite; every finite selected support bound is <=log J. More precisely with the native VFormula (PT duality, local Tate duality and finiteness of local/global H^0), write a_l(e)=length H^0(G_S,Q_l/Z_l(-e)), b_l(e)=sum_v length H^0(G_v,Q_l/Z_l(-e)). All are finite (local and global cyclotomic images open, e>0). Then
    length Sha^1 = v_l(|A_e|) + a_l(e) - b_l(e).
The correction is nonpositive since restriction of invariants into any one local factor is injective and S_l nonempty. Hence log I_D = log J(E,t) - sum_{e,l} (b_l(e)-a_l(e)) log l, with finite nonnegative correction supported on primes dividing J. This identifies the original budget with a K-group index minus an explicit local invariant correction, not the geometric finite-place quotient from 1338d45c.
