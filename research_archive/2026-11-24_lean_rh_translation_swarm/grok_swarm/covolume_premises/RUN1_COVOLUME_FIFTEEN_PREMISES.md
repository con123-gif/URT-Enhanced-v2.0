Two of them give constants A, B for the loss estimate, and two give C1, C2 ≥ 0. Fm.ez > 0, because the scale is positive at a chosen index. `exists_loss_le` then gives a threshold x0 where the loss is at most Fm.ez/7. Here unitE ≥ 0, from the size condition, so for every C ≥ C0 the bound mainT + C·unitE follows by `mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_right` and linear arithmetic. ∎ (This step is elementary. All the content sits in the eight conditions.)
