## 3. Exact obstruction to skipping the integral cotangent step
A tempting replacement of model_upper is: finite degree-zero algebra plus a nonzero trace difference at every two-signed generic point implies a bound on integral negative cotangent length by the product of generic trace differences. This inference without additional premises is false. For any m>=1 take a DVR O with uniformizer pi and the graded augmented O-algebra R=O[X]/(pi^m X,X^2), deg X=-1, augmentation X->0. It is finite as O-module, R_0=O, and its generic fiber is just Frac(O). There are no two-signed points and the condition of nonzero evaluation on them is vacuous even for trace element a=0 in degree zero. The empty trace product is 1. Nevertheless the augmentation ideal I satisfies I^2=0 and
    length_O((I/I^2)_{-1}) = length_O(O/(pi^m)) = m.
This follows from the direct decomposition O ⊕ (O/pi^m)X, with the displayed multiplication. The length is arbitrarily large while generic trace data are unchanged. Thus generic-point data alone cannot replace the native integral cotangent and rigidity obligations. This is an exact algebra counterexample to a proposed weak-premise inference, not a counterexample to LemmaM or an assertion that this ring is a native deformation model. A legitimate determinant replacement must prove an integral length-control bridge using the actual M1–M5 (or separately proved stronger premises), not simply compute the determinant of a reduced generic algebra. This identifies a precise next mathematical question rather than adding another RH-equivalent inequality.
