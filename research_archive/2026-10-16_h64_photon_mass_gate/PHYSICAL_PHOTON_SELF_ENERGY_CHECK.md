# Photon self-energy test on the recovered H64 charged-lepton source (2026-10-16)

This is an actual mass-dependent perturbative photon-loop benchmark, not an optical phase postulate or complete URT mass prediction.

Recovered October 8 gauge-covariant 48-state lepton operator has charged chiral singular values
[0.6001226184580051, 1.2089634740699797, 1.7277091388383088],
with Q=sum(sigma^2)/(sum sigma)^2=0.3842632706743234.
The photon charge on its six charged states is -I6 and commutes exactly with this matrix.
It follows that universal photon coupling permits eigenvalue-dependent self-energies, but a common scalar propagation phase cannot change mass ratios.

Standard one-loop QED gamma_m=3 alpha/(2pi), with conditional internal alpha=1/137.035999178195, gives gamma_m=0.00348422919626. One-loop pole/MS conversion at a common scale gives Q=0.3839598928955856. Reversing the sign of the logarithmic correction for a diagnostic gives Q=0.3845679257176304. Neither is remotely 2/3. The dimensionless pure-power correction sigma_i->sigma_i^p would need p=3.9318637062 for Q=2/3; this would formally require alpha>6 at one-loop, beyond its validity.

This is a test of the **conditional archived source and ordinary perturbative QED**, not a no-go for the complete nonperturbative URT history theory. The archive does not identify a single full selected H64 photon-history momentum vertex with its charged pole mass operator. Bare A4 speed 1/sqrt(2) is a coordinate-convention velocity, not evidence of actual vacuum photon slowdown. The correct physical task is a gauge-invariant, species-dressed Dyson/Feshbach pole calculation from the same selected action.
