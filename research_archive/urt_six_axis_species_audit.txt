Consequences
------------
1. Minimum Frobenius information alone selects the golden class, but every
   golden block leaves one of the four right-handed species directions
   completely uncoupled.

2. If recursive closure requires all four right-handed directions to
   participate, the golden class is inadmissible and the equal class is
   selected. It has exactly four nonzero arrows: two from each left direction.

3. The seed does not uniquely determine how the portal algebra couples to
   these arrows. The space of A5-equivariant linear maps

       (1 + 3 + 5) -> End(S6)

   has dimension 7.

   After choosing the order-two grading, the residual V4-equivariant map space

       (1 + 3 + 5) -> Hom(S_L, S_R)

   has dimension 16.

Therefore the six-axis carrier derives the 2L+4R count and a canonical
full-support four-arrow incidence class, but it does not by itself derive
four unique generation matrices.
