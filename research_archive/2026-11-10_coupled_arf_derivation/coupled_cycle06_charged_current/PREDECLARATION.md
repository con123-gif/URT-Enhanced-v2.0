# Coupled cycle 06 predeclaration — charged current dimension

Count A5-equivariant, gauge-covariant maps from the two hidden channels
4_3 (the image of 3⊗5) and 4_5 (the image of Sym² 5) into 90-edge
1-cochains of the original five-solid complex, and those that couple to
H13 vertex matter by an edge-supported pairing, with Ward contacts.

Definitions, fixed before computing:

- Signed 1-cochains C^1(solid) are antisymmetric functions on oriented
  edges. Divergence is the original incidence b1 (vertex–edge). Conserved
  currents are ker(b1).
- A5 is the 60-element rotation group of the icosahedron, generated by
  enumerating SO(3) maps that permute the 12 archived icosahedral vertices
  (simply transitive on vertex-neighbor flags). The same 60 rotations act
  on icosahedral and dodecahedral cells (the dual pair) and are tested on
  tetrahedron, cube and octahedron vertex sets.
- Hom dimensions are character inner products independently recomputed
  from fixed-cell counts, and checked against the numerical intertwiner
  rank where cheap (ico only).
- H13 = centre ⊕ 12 icosahedral vertices. An edge-supported pairing is
  the image of unoriented ico edges inside Sym²(C^{12}) (commutative
  Weyl-scalar pairing) or of oriented edges inside Λ²(C^{12})
  (anticommuting pairing). The centre does not incident any 190-edge.
- Ward contact for a 1-current: the current lies in ker(b1).
  Charge contact for a pairing to a charge-+2 hidden source: vertex
  charge +1 (already selected in isolated cycle 01); this is used only
  as a filter, not to insert an ARF.
- tetra / cube / octa receive no geometric A5-action unless the 60
  rotations preserve their vertex sets (to be checked). If not, the only
  remaining A5-action is the trivial one, and Hom(4, trivial) = 0.

Will report each Hom dimension and the surviving dimension after Ward.
Classification: PROVED with an obstruction if the Ward-surviving dimension
is not 1; PROVED unique if it is 1; FALSIFIED if a construction identity
fails. No ARF target is used.
