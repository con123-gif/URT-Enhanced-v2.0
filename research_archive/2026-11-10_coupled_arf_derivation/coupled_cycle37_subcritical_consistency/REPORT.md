T_c(γ) ≈ γ²/(4 c_even) ≈ 28.1 ≈ 4.7 η (cycle 33’s high-T
formula). None of these assignments is performed here.

## No identification (H1)

Cycle 22 proved that y_even is FREE: unit-norm histories,
Z_hist, KMS data (η, ρ★) and the charge-2 Ward identity do
not fix a value above criticality. No original-Cathedral or
coupled-line source identifies the even Yukawa with Δ, Δ²,
γ, √Δ or c_even. Making any of those identifications would
be a new postulate, not a derivation.

(The smallness of y_c itself is already explained: y_c(η) =
Δ √(c5/χ(η)) ≈ 0.005940, so the threshold is of order Δ.
That does not set the Yukawa equal to Δ.)

## Conclusion (H2, H3)

The subcritical branch is a mathematically consistent phase
of the archived action, and its T = η kernel is a
parameter-free, positive, nearly Maxwell-like form with a
near-zero London mass (cycles 32 and 36). It is not selected
by any derived coupling. Occupying it requires an extra
postulate that sets y_even < y_c(η) ≈ 0.006. The only derived
temperature together with any O(1) Yukawa selects the
supercritical, London-massive branch (cycle 33).
