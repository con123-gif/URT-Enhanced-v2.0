## What would count as a unique derived 4D coframe

A unique construction, already present in the proved operators or
uniquely named by the archive, that does all of the following
with no extra postulate:

1. Supplies a 4-dimensional spacetime geometry (a 4-manifold, a
   4-complex, or a 3-geometry plus a derived time direction), not
   merely a 2-complex or a 3-sphere.
2. Equips that geometry with a unique coframe / vierbein / volume
   form, up to overall scale, so that the Maxwell quadratic form
   is (K/4) ∫ F² in those units, with K unique.
3. Does not rely on the C_24 circulant product already tested in
   cycle 40, and does not choose spherical DEC (or any other
   cycle-47 named metric) on the grounds that a Rayleigh quotient
   on S² approaches ℓ(ℓ+1)=2.

If several inequivalent 4D constructions exist, or if each
candidate is missing a unique time, a unique 3-geometry, or a
unique symbol matching, there is no unique coframe. The following
objects will be examined and will not be promoted after the fact:

- The derived spatial 2-complex (homeomorphic to S²) times a time
  direction. A derived KMS period 1/η, if used as a circumference,
  still needs a unique slicing and a unique identification of
  KMS time with spacetime time.

  fibration S³ → S².
- H64 as the 6-cube, and the 4-dimensional A5-irreps 4_3, 4_5 as
  internal spaces.
- The five-solid Euclidean embeddings in ℝ³, jointly or with
  Cbridge.
- A 24-cell, an F_5-plane, or a 13⁴ torus, unless the archive
  uniquely derives one as the spacetime coframe (it is not
  expected to).
- The cycle-40 C_24 product (not reused) and the cycle-47
  spherical DEC star (not selected).
