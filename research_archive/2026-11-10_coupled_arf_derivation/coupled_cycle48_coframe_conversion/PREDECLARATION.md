## What would count as a unique derived conversion

A unique conversion must, from already proved structure and from
statements already in the archive, fix a single number Z such
that K_γ = Z · κ_♦, where κ_♦ is a uniquely identified discrete
coefficient, with no remaining choice of:

- which discrete avatar is κ_♦ (κ_iso, κ_REM, Tr Π_T / Tr Δ1_T,

- which Hodge inner product, among the A5-invariant lumped
  family of cycle 47, supplies Δ1 and Π;
- which Jacobian matches the discrete quadratic form to the
  continuum symbol (K_γ/4)∫F² (coframe / vierbein / volume

  kinematic identity (1/16)Σ_{a<b}|F_ab|² = |k|²|A_3|² −

- which continuum background the symbol is written on
  (S² versus a 4D spacetime coframe).

If any of those remains a free convention, or if the archive
explicitly leaves the relevant factor as a missing input, there
is no unique conversion. A match of some combination to 1/137.036
or to R_α is not a derivation of Z.
