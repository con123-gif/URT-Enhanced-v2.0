## Hypotheses
H1. Hom(Sym² 3, 4) = 0, and there is no 3' source to feed 3⊗3'. Thus F is unique up to two overall scales. Those two scales are source normalisations, not fermion Yukawa ratios.
H2. First and second U(1) Ward identities already vanish on the whole residual Yukawa space (cycle 01 / alt_cycle09). The seagull contact comes from the covariantised Hodge kinetic; once that kinetic is fixed to a ray, the seagull is determined and does not constrain (α, β, γ, δ).

The projected product x * y = P((C x) ⊙ (C y)) leaves the domain 3 ⊕ 5 (outputs live in 4_3 ⊕ 4_5), so F cannot be iterated as a binary algebra on 3 ⊕ 5. No Jacobi-type closure on 3 ⊕ 5 is available to cut Yukawas.
H4. The Schur-reduced hidden potential is positive definite in (h3, h5) (alt_cycle03). Composed with F it is a nonnegative quartic in (t, f) and is therefore bounded below, independently of (α, β, γ, δ). Stability of V_hist ∘ F does not restrict Yukawa ratios.
