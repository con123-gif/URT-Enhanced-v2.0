# Predeclaration — alt_cycle38: π-flux combined with the spinor lift

Date: 2026-11-10 (Europe/London)
Route: (38) Combine π-flux with the spinor lift: per-face spinor holonomy 2 cos(π/10) times −1. Does the combined connection break A4 (e.g. to ℤ3 or ℤ2, or T) and split the pairing multiplets into nondegenerate singlets? Find the derived vacuum of the combined system (fermion-only, as in alt34).

## Setup
S1. The spin connection of alt_cycle33 is the principal SU(2) parallel transport on vertex12 ⊗ 2, with triangular face holonomy of trace 2 cos(π/10). The π-flux of alt_cycles 31–35 is A_e ≡ π on every icosahedral edge, so each edge of the spin Laplacian is multiplied by −1. A triangular face therefore picks up (−1)^3 = −1, and the combined face holonomy has trace −2 cos(π/10).

S2. Pairing remains Δ_spin = Δ_spatial ⊗ ε at the even A4 condensate of alt_cycle25, with the spinor-scale r_* of alt_cycle33 when the spinor theory is used, and with r_* of alt_cycle25 when a scalar control is quoted.

S3. The derived vacuum is the minimiser of the fermion-only BdG free energy of this combined system (no external Maxwell term), as in alt_cycle34’s spinor sector: Ω at A = 0 versus A = π, together with the sign of the transverse Hessian already computed there. Additional seeds (random, gradient) are evaluated; no new parameter is introduced.

S4. Stabiliser: the 60 A5 rotations (and, where it matters, their SU(2) lifts) acting on face holonomies of the combined connection and on Δ_spin. Time reversal is A ↦ −A together with U ↦ U*; because π ≡ −π it preserves the U(1) flux, and the question is whether the combined holonomy pattern is equal to its conjugate. Breaking to ℤ3, ℤ2 or a chiral T is reported only if the computed stabiliser is that group.

S5. Spectrum: positive BdG energies at the derived vacuum, grouped at relative 10^{-5} (the Laplacian / finite-r threshold of alt_cycle26) and also listed raw. Three nondegenerate charged singlets means at least three distinct positive levels, all of multiplicity one, with no leftover multiplicity ≥ 2 after that grouping. This route does not compare C_mass or R_mass (route 39 is the place for that, and only if such a spectrum appears).

## Hypotheses
H1. The π-flux pattern is A5-invariant and the spin connection is 2I-covariant, so the combined connection does not reduce the A4 (or its binary cover 2T) already selected by Δ. Time reversal does not pick a U(1) handedness (combined traces are real). The stabiliser is not ℤ3 or ℤ2.
H2. Pairing multiplets therefore remain multiplets (the large-r pattern 2 ⊕ 6 ⊕ 12 ⊕ 4 of alt_cycle33, possibly with O(||K||/r) fine structure), not three nondegenerate singlets.
H3. The fermion-only vacuum of the combined system is the π-flux phase (alt_cycle34 already found Ω(π) < Ω(0) and a negative-definite transverse Hessian for the spinor sector).
