## Spectrum protocol
S1. The joint vacuum of alt_cycle41 (even channel, y = 1, T = η, primary kinetic K0 = L_ico ⊕ L_dodeca, and the Cbridge control KC) supplies a single pairing Δ_32(h_*). Positive BdG energies of the 32-site problem are grouped at relative 10^{-5} (the Laplacian / finite-r threshold of alt_cycle26).

S2. Three nondegenerate charged singlets means at least three distinct positive grouped levels, each of multiplicity one, with no leftover multiplicity ≥ 2 among those three. (A residual unpaired near-zero, if present, is reported separately and is not one of the three.) This requires the joint stabiliser to be small enough that A4-triplets split (stabiliser C3, C2, C2×C2, or 1). If the joint stabiliser is A4 (or larger), A4-triplets cannot split and this comparison is not run.

## Predeclared ratios (written before any mass number is inspected)

    ρ21 = m2 / m1,   ρ31 = m3 / m1,   ρ32 = m3 / m2,
    Q_Koide = (m1 + m2 + m3) / (√m1 + √m2 + √m3)²,
    Q_rms = (m1 + m2 + m3) / √(m1² + m2² + m3²).
    These five numbers, and no fitted scale, are the comparison inputs.

S4. Historical targets (named inputs, not inserted into the model):

S5. If three nondegenerate charged singlets do not appear, C_mass and R_mass are not compared and T1 is not run.
