# Predeclaration — alt_cycle18: minimal A5 breaking for three distinct charged masses

Date: 2026-11-10 (Europe/London)
Route: (18) The three-distinct-masses obstruction: find which coupled Cathedral structure among the 24-clock, the KMS state ρ*, the 2I lift, and the five-solid bridge breaks A5 minimally so as to give three distinct charged masses without new parameters; prove or falsify.

## Premises
P1. The one-loop self-energy on H13 is A5-block-diagonal with forced degeneracies (1, 1, 3, 3, 5) and massless singlets (alt_cycle04, alt_cycle15). Three nondegenerate charged masses require either three independent singlet eigenvalues or a breaking of at least one of the multiplets 3, 3', 5 down to singlets, without introducing free continuous parameters.
P2. Structures tested, each used only as already constructed in previous cycles / the archive:
    (a) 24-clock Laplacian L2 with constant gold-angle holonomy, coupled by the product operator K_space ⊗ I_clock + I_space ⊗ L_clock (the archived clock coupling is the same for every spatial component).
    (b) KMS / hidden weight ρ* = (I_4 ⊕ Δ² I_4) / [4(1+Δ²)], which rescales the two hidden quartets and is a multiple of the identity on each.
    (c) The 2I = SL(2,5) lift H13 ⊗ 2, with the character table of alt_cycle01.
    (d) The five-solid bridge restricted to icosahedral vertices, Cbridge = 4 P_1 + 4 P_3 (alt_cycle12), alone and added to the Hodge Laplacian.

## Hypotheses
H1. Each of (a)–(d) commutes with A5 on the spatial fermion carrier (or, for (c), is 2I-equivariant). None of them splits the A5 multiplets 3, 3', 5 into singlets.
H2. The product / joint use of (a), (b) and (d) remains A5-equivariant and preserves the degeneracies (up to copying the same clock spectrum onto every spatial partner).
H3. The 2I lift has no 1-dimensional irrep (H13 ⊗ 2 ≅ 3·2 ⊕ 2·4' ⊕ 2·6); it enlarges rather than splits degeneracies.
H4. Therefore none of these four structures produces three distinct nondegenerate charged masses without an additional A5-breaking choice (for example, selecting one of the six five-fold axes, whose restriction 3 → 1 ⊕ 2 over ℝ is not canonically picked by (a)–(d)).
