## What would count as a derived principle

A derived principle must, from proved coupled-line structure
and/or from a unique archival statement, do both of the
following with no extra postulate and no reference to measured
α or to R_α:

1. Select a single microscopic U(1) generator among the
   candidates already catalogued in cycle 31 (finite-algebra
   u(1); 16-carrier / H64 species charge Q = Y+T_3; Hodge–
   Maxwell connection on the 30 edges as a *charge* generator,
   not merely as the home of A; the five-orientation splitter
   Q = 2C/√15; coupled-line Peierls Q_geo), or uniquely name
   a new generator already present in the proved operators.
2. Fix the electron-unit convention: which state or representation
   carries charge −1 in that generator’s units, equivalently
   why the Thomson chart is Q_e = −1 rather than another integer
   or another overall scale.

If several candidates remain, or if the archive still labels
the relevant map as a candidate, a matching condition, or an
unfinished closure obligation, there is no derived principle.
The working uniqueness of the charge-0 Hodge–Noether current
at r = 0 (cycle 43, conditional on Q_geo) is not, by itself,
a selection of Q_geo as U(1)_EM; that conditionality is what
this cycle is testing.
