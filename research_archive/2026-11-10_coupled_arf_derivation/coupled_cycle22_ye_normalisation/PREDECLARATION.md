# Coupled cycle 22 predeclaration — is the overall y_even scale fixed?

Question C of this block: cycle 20 showed that pairing-dominated ratios
are parameter-free, while ratios that mix a pairing gap with a
ye-independent energy (the H13 mode E = 13, the odd hidden mass
√(2 c_odd), Laplacian scales) still depend on the overall y_even
through g_* = y_e r_* = y_e² N / (4 c_even). This cycle asks whether
any history-derived normalisation already present in the archived
theory uniquely fixes that scale.

No new coupling is introduced. The four named candidates, and only
these, will be examined:

1. Unit-norm histories / visible sources. The map F : Sym²(ℝ⁸) →
   4_3 ⊕ 4_5 of cycle 03, restricted to unit-norm visible 5 (and to
   unit-norm 3 ⊕ 5), produces a bounded set of hidden amplitudes.
   Record max ||h5|| on those unit spheres and the ye that would be
   required to match the condensed r_* = ||h5||_unit via
   r_* = y_e N / (4 c_even). If that ye lies below y_c,even, the
   identification “condensate = F(unit-norm source)” is incompatible
   with condensation and cannot be used to fix ye in the condensed
   phase. The Yukawa ye is not itself a history amplitude.

2. The 64-history partition function. Z_hist(β) =
   2 + 12 e^{−10β} + 30 e^{−16β} + 20 e^{−18β} (cycle 17) does not
   depend on ye. Z_hist(0) = 64 and Z_hist(η) = 2 are counts /
   Boltzmann sums in the history complex. The fermion grand potential
   Ω_* ≈ − y_e² N² / (16 c_even) does depend on ye, but equating
   Z_fermion or e^{−Ω_*/T} to Z_hist (or to 64) is an extra matching
   postulate, not an identity of the coupled action (the two sectors
   are independently normalised). Counterfactual ye values obtained
   by imposing those matchings will be recorded only to show that
   different named targets disagree; they will not be adopted.

3. KMS normalisation at T = η. The archived KMS data are the
   temperature T = η itself and the hidden metric
   ρ★ = (I4 ⊕ Δ² I4)/[4(1+Δ²)]. Both are independent of ye. The
   state-normalisation Tr ρ = 1 is automatic and does not fix ye.

4. Ward normalisation of the charge-2 vertex. The pairing bilinear
   picks up e^{i(λ_i+λ_j)} and hopping picks up e^{i(λ_j−λ_i)}
   (cycle 10 / alt29). The charge extracted from those phases is 2,
   independently of |Δ| and of ye. Cycle 12 already found that the
   geometric U(1) Ward identities hold for every (y_o, y_e) and do
   not constrain the Yukawa plane. A Ward identity is homogeneous in
   ye (both sides scale together) and cannot fix its magnitude.

Classification, predeclared: ye is FIXED if and only if exactly one
of (1)–(4) produces a unique ye > y_c,even without an extra postulate,
and the others are compatible with that value. Otherwise ye is FREE
(PROVED). No ARF is used.
