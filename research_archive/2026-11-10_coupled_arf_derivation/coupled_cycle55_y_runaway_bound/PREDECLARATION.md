# Coupled cycle 55 predeclaration — does any derived term
# already in the coupled machine bound the y-runaway?

Date: 2026-11-10 (Europe/London)

Cycle 52 found that the full Nambu-plus-gauge free energy at
T = η, if minimised in y_even as an extra postulate, runs away
to large y as ω(y) ~ −N² y²/(16 c_even), with no interior
minimum on the frozen sample. This cycle asks whether any term
*already derived* in the coupled machine supplies a bound that
stops that runaway, or else names the obstruction.

No new Maxwell term and no new tree |D_A Δ|² are introduced
(the archived one-loop action has neither; cycles 28–29 and
SYNTHESIS obstruction 4). No coframe is invented, no Hodge
weighting is selected, and the cycle-47 spread is not hunted.
y_π is not located or used as a selector. No historical ARF is
inserted. The `newtons-cathedral` tip remains read-only at
`6bad7b3`. Predeclaration is hashed before the catalogue is
scored. No new y-sample is introduced; numbers already computed
in cycle 52 may be reused.

## Predeclared catalogue (terms already in the machine)

A candidate bounds the runaway only if it is already present
as a derived term or identity of the coupled action, and if,
once included, ω(y) at the derived vacuum has a unique interior
minimum at a finite y, or else becomes unbounded below only
outside a derived compact domain for y. Matching ω(y) to an
y-independent quantity (Z_hist, a unit-norm history, a Ward
identity) is the cycle-22 normalisation test, already shown
not to fix y > y_c, and is not a potential that bounds a
variational runaway.

B1. The cycle-52 functional itself,
    Ω = V_hist + Ω_f(K[A], Δ(y,r), T=η),
    minimised over r and A ∈ {0, π}. Already computed: runaway
    ~ −N² y²/(16 c_even). The large-vev remainder
    Ω − (−N² y²/(16 c_even)) will be read off the existing
    supercritical sample {0.1, 1.0, 2.0} only. A bound would
    require a remainder that grows at least as +y² and turns
    ω upward on that sample. An O(1) or slowly growing
    remainder does not bound a −y² leading term.

B2. A tree Maxwell term (K/4)∫F² or a tree |D_A Δ|². Included
    only if already present in the archived coupled action.
    They are not expected; introducing them is excluded.

B3. History and modular normalisations already tested in
    cycles 17 and 22: Z_hist(β) (independent of y), unit-norm
    visible sources, KMS ρ★, and the charge-2 Ward identity
    (homogeneous in y). These are not a potential for y and
    do not bound a runaway of Ω(y).

B4. Resolvent / transfer poles of T0 and of the cycle-13
    bubble chain, and spectral-radius consistency of the
    iteration at a derived vacuum. T0 poles are independent
    of y (cycle 52). A demand that some pole lie inside a
    fixed disk, or that a photon pole be massless, is not a
    derived bound and will not be used. The falsified Dirac
    pairing kernel of cycle 13 is not recomputed as a new
    potential.

B5. Induced one-loop Π_T (cycles 25, 29, 32): a quadratic form
    on the connection A, not a potential for the Yukawa y.
    At large vev it is a London mass 1/12, independent of
    further increase of y once the system is condensed.

B6. The finite 12-site graph as a putative UV cutoff. A bound
    would require the pairing amplitude r, or Ω_f, to be
    unable to grow with y. If BdG energies remain real and
    grow with the pairing scale s = y r, Ω_f becomes more
    negative, which does not cap y.

B7. The odd channel y_odd and the hidden stiffness
    V = c3||h3||² + c_even||h5||². The stiffness is already
    inside Ω (V_hist). y_odd = 0 is the derived O(1) choice
    (the odd channel does not condense at O(1) Yukawa); no
    derived +y_even⁴ term is expected from leaving y_odd on.

B8. Hbits / 24-clock / five-solid terms already shown to be
    y-independent (Z_hist; ρ★; the history complex). No new
    clock–Yukawa coupling is invented.

## Hypotheses

H1. B1 does not bound the runaway: the leading term is −y²
    and the cycle-52 remainder does not turn ω upward.
H2. B2 is absent from the archived action; introducing it is
    not allowed. B3–B8 supply no derived potential or compact
    domain that caps y.
H3. Therefore no derived term already in the coupled machine
    bounds the y-runaway. The obstruction is the absence of
    such a term, together with the already-computed −y²
    leading behaviour of the existing functional. y_even
    remains free. No ARF is compared.
