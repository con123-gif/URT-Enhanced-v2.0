FIGURE 4 FIRST-PASS QUANTITATIVE AUDIT
======================================

Source:
Published Figure 4 inset from:
Mbah et al., Early-stage bifurcation of crystallization in a sphere,
Nature Communications 14, 5299 (2023).

Method:
The blue fitted curve was traced from the published image by a continuity-
constrained dynamic-programming path. Axes were calibrated from the labelled
600-800 simulation-time range and the 100%, 60%, and 20% probability levels.
A four-parameter decreasing logistic curve was fitted.

Fit:
  lower asymptote                = -0.01035
  upper asymptote                = 0.93775
  midpoint t*                    = 717.649
  logistic scale                 = 8.612
  10%-90% transition width       = 37.846
  midpoint slope                 = -0.02752 probability / t*
  odds multiplier per t*         = 0.89037

URT comparison:
  candidate kappa                = 12/13 = 0.92307692
  one 12/13 odds contraction
  corresponds to                 = 0.689 t*

Important:
The conversion from outcome probability to a URT pressure/defect variable has
not been derived. Therefore the odds comparison is diagnostic only, not a
confirmation of kappa=12/13.

The principal empirical result is the sharp geometric bifurcation:
the fitted midpoint is near t*=718, while the paper identifies t*=720 as the
point at which five grains form a pentagonal-bipyramid nucleus and the pathway
becomes committed to decahedral closure.
