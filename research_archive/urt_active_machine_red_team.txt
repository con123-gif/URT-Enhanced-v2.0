2. WHAT IS RIGOROUS
-------------------
For a locally modified matrix A, polar_close(A) is the unique nearest unitary
matrix when A is nonsingular:

    polar(A) = argmin_{Q^*Q=I} ||Q-A||_F^2.

Embedding matrices as equal-covariance isotropic Gaussian response means makes
this equivalent to a KL minimum-change projection. Thus the closure/projection
part of the active gate is mathematically valid.

3. WHAT IS NOT DERIVED
----------------------

    3 quark gates + 4 lepton gates = 14 real controls.

The eight reported flavour diagnostics are:

    Vus, Vcb, Vub, J_CKM,
    sin^2(theta12), sin^2(theta23), sin^2(theta13), J_PMNS.

A central-difference Jacobian of these eight outputs with respect to the 14 gate
controls at the reported solution has:

    total rank   = 8
    total nullity= 6

Sector by sector:

    quarks:  6 controls -> 4 observables, rank 4, nullity 2
    leptons: 8 controls -> 4 observables, rank 4, nullity 4

Therefore the gate family is locally capable of independently fitting every
reported observable, with six remaining unconstrained directions. Removing
observed numbers from the final source code does not make the construction a
blind prediction because the gate formulae were reverse-identified from the
residuals before being written as Cathedral-constant combinations.
