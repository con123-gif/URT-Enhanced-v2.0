## 0. Executive statement

The project began from the claim that classical Navier–Stokes can mathematically follow trajectories that are not physically realized because real turbulent dynamics is bounded by a deeper selection principle. Subsequent URT work sharpened that principle into an exact scale–phase dynamical manifold,

\[
r_{k+1}=\frac{\pi}{e}r_k,
\qquad
\theta_{k+1}=\theta_k+\frac{2\pi}{\phi^2},
\]

or, equivalently,

\[
z_{k+1}=qz_k,
\qquad
q=\frac{\pi}{e}e^{2\pi i/\phi^2}.
\]

For the inward orientation the physical length scale contracts by

\[
s=\frac e\pi=0.8652559794\ldots.
\]

The new OpenAI forced Navier–Stokes construction supplies an explicit continuum blow-up mechanism with shrinking core scale and diverging velocity. When this scaling is sampled on URT scale steps, the singular branch requires amplification at each inward scale transition, whereas canonical URT requires contraction at large state amplitude. Thus the singular continuum orbit is asymptotically incompatible with canonical URT under the physical mode/scale identification.

The harder question is whether one can remove the external forcing and obtain an **unforced** Navier–Stokes singularity using the same structural ideas. The work in this note isolates a candidate route:

1. use the ordinary unforced quadratic Navier–Stokes nonlinearity to generate the next-scale pulse internally;
2. choose parent wavevectors so the child wavenumber ratio is exactly \(\pi/e\) in continuous Fourier space;
3. choose polarizations so the desired sum mode survives with large coupling while the principal difference mode cancels under the Leray projector;
4. localize the waves by curl potentials, preserving exact incompressibility while making localization leakage summable;
5. use the growing homogeneous pulse direction already present in the forced singular construction to amplify an internally generated child seed;
6. relay peak × tail interactions from one pulse to the next rather than externally seeding each new pulse;
7. carry correction cycles to infinity in a shrinking-radius Gevrey norm rather than stopping with a flat residual;
8. formulate the complete mechanism directly on \(\mathbb T^3\) to avoid global spatial cutoff forcing;
9. prove that the full directed parent–relay–child interaction algebra closes inside the existing wave/mean correction classes.

Items 1–8 now have explicit algebraic or scaling support. Item 9 is the remaining PDE closure problem.

## 12. Why preloading all future pulses at time zero is the wrong mechanism

A naive construction would encode every future high-frequency pulse directly into the initial datum. For positive viscosity this faces a severe heat penalty: a high-frequency mode required only near late time is damped by a factor

\[
e^{-\nu k^2t}.
\]

To recover a prescribed late amplitude by backward preloading would require exponentially larger initial coefficients, conflicting with smooth finite-energy data.

The correct causal mechanism is instead:

\[
\boxed{
\text{parent pulse decays}
\to
\text{nonlinear interaction creates child late}
\to
\text{background shear amplifies child}
\to
\text{child becomes next parent}.
}
\]

Each new high-frequency packet is generated only shortly before it is needed.

## 14. Localized divergence-free packets and leakage

A global plane wave cannot produce the required physical concentration. Localize while preserving incompressibility by a curl potential.

For slowly varying envelope \(A(x)\) on length \(L\), set

\[
b_p=i\,p\times a

and

\[

=
\nabla\times\left[
\frac{A(x)}{K}b_p e^{iKp\cdot x}
\right].
\]

Then exactly

\[
\nabla\cdot W_p=0,
\]

and asymptotically

\[
W_p
=
A(x)a e^{iKp\cdot x}
+
O((KL)^{-1}).
\]

For two such packets, the desired \(p+r\) child interaction has principal size

\[
-iK C_{\rm triad}A^2 e^{iK(p+r)\cdot x},

while the principal difference mode remains canceled and reappears only through localization errors.

Thus

\[
\frac{\text{parasitic localized interaction}}

=

Using the singular construction's scales

\[

\boxed{

Localization therefore introduces a summable defect rather than an accumulating order-one error.

## 16. Hyperbolic packet shadowing mechanism

\[

=

=

The technical issue is not the abstract shadowing mechanism itself; it is proving that the **full modified Navier–Stokes relay system** has the required uniform packet splitting after all intended and parasitic interactions are included.

## 18. Infinite correction in a shrinking-radius Gevrey norm

The forced construction performs repeated correction cycles and gains a positive decay exponent at every cycle but eventually stops and leaves a residual that is flat to all algebraic orders.

To obtain exact \(f=0\) closure, the proposed modification is to continue the correction to infinity in a Gevrey norm.

For Gevrey order \(\sigma>1\), define schematically

\[
\|f\|_{\rho,s,\sigma}^2
=
\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z^d}
 e^{2\rho\langle k\rangle^{1/\sigma}}
\langle k\rangle^{2s}
|\widehat f(k)|^2.
\]

Gevrey classes with \(\sigma>1\) admit compactly supported bump functions, so localization remains available.

If an inverse loses a polynomial factor in frequency, then shrinking the radius from \(\rho\) to \(\rho-\Delta\rho\) absorbs that loss at cost

\[
(\Delta\rho)^{-M}.
\]

Choose stagewise radius loss

\[
\Delta\rho_j
=\frac{c_\rho}{(j+1)^2}.
\]

Then

\[
\sum_j\Delta\rho_j<\infty,
\]

so a positive Gevrey radius remains:

\[
\rho_\infty
=
\rho_0-
\sum_j\Delta\rho_j>0.
\]

If each full correction cycle gains a factor comparable to

\[
\varepsilon^{1/10},
\qquad 0<\varepsilon<1,
\]

then stage \(j\) carries

\[
\varepsilon^{j/10}.
\]

The inverse-radius penalty is polynomial in \(j\), hence

## 26. What has been ruled out or corrected

The following earlier ideas should **not** be carried forward as established claims:

7. **“The unforced problem is solved.”** It is not yet. The relay interaction algebra and uniform exact closure must still be proved.
