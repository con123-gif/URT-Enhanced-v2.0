## Exact 5-pair dynamical structure

For each tidal pair, let

\[
G=\begin{pmatrix}-6\Gamma&-\omega\\\omega&-2\Gamma\end{pmatrix},\quad
\omega=2\pi/\phi^2,\quad\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2.
\]

The matrix \(H=G+4\Gamma I\) satisfies \(H^2=(4\Gamma^2-\omega^2)I\). In the oscillatory regime \(\omega>2\Gamma\), with \(\nu=\sqrt{\omega^2-4\Gamma^2}\),

\[
e^{Gt}=e^{-4\Gamma t}\left[\cos(\nu t)I+\frac{\sin(\nu t)}{\nu}(G+4\Gamma I)\right].
\]

The ordinary 2x2 real matrix algebra generated by a skew square-minus-identity rotation and two symmetric square-plus-identity matrices is a split-quaternion/Cl(1,1) algebra, not Hamilton's division quaternions.

## The Lyapunov claim and 9:5 ratio

The unique solution of \(G^TP+PG=-\chi I/2\), for \(\Gamma>0\), has

\[
P_{ee}=\frac{\chi(8\Gamma^2+\omega^2)}{16\Gamma(12\Gamma^2+\omega^2)},\quad
P_{bb}=\frac{\chi(24\Gamma^2+\omega^2)}{16\Gamma(12\Gamma^2+\omega^2)},\quad
P_{eb}=\frac{\chi\omega}{8(12\Gamma^2+\omega^2)}.
\]

At \(\Gamma_*=\omega/\sqrt{12}\), \(P_{bb}/P_{ee}=9/5\) exactly, and \(|\operatorname{Re}\lambda/\operatorname{Im}\lambda|=\sqrt2\). **However**, the Lyapunov equation does not uniquely choose \(\Gamma_*\). It maximizes the chosen flux proxy \(\Gamma P_{eb}\); \(P_{eb}\) alone is strictly decreasing with \(\Gamma\).

## Why the single electric release is 3:7, not 9:5

For a pure-electric initial state \(v_0=(\sqrt2,0)\), set \(Q=\int_0^\infty e^{Gt}v_0v_0^Te^{G^Tt}\,dt\). The matrix obeys \(GQ+QG^T=-v_0v_0^T\). Its ratio is

\[
\frac{\int b^2dt}{\int e^2dt}=\frac{Q_{bb}}{Q_{ee}}=\frac{\omega^2}{16\Gamma^2+\omega^2},
\]

which becomes exactly **3/7** at the proposed matching rate, not 9/5. For the default example the magnetic squared amplitude peaks at approximately \(t=0.314\), reaching 0.17537 while the electric squared amplitude is 0.05855.

Setting \(\dot E_{\rm reservoir}=-\dot{\mathcal F}\) ensures algebraic conservation but **defines** the reservoir coupling; a Laplacian's uniform null mode does not automatically receive this energy.
