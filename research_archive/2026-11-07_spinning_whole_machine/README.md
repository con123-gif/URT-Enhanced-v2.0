# Spinning geometry and whole-Cathedral computations (7 November 2026)
Nine original standalone Python scripts recovered from the saved ChatGPT Library / conversation-created artifacts on 8 November 2026. They contain overlapping approaches and model choices, **not nine independent physical derivations**.
## Scripts
- `cathedral_all_in_one.py`: combined five-Platonic Hodge / hidden sector / URT 24-track construction.
- `cathedral_derived_physics_step.py`: tetrahedral cubic coupling, orientation-dependent spectral projectors, Berry curvature / quantum metric and rotating five-solid energy.
- `cathedral_integrated_verification.py`: independent finite operator and stability checks.
- `cathedral_spin_dynamics.py`: angular dynamics; identifies model dependence of torque/damping.
- `cathedral_spin_first_principles_audit.py`: source/frequency selection and limits.
- `cathedral_spinning_selection.py`: bounded angular-mode selection model.
- `cathedral_spinning_test.py`: rotating contact and projected coupling tests.
- `cathedral_urt_chiral_derivation.py`: coupled geometric/chiral 3–5 checks.
- `urt_spinning_interaction_law.py`: proposed five-solid interaction and gradients.
## Reproducibility and status
All nine scripts were executed on 8 November 2026 and exited 0 with assertions satisfied under the current Python/numpy/scipy/sympy/networkx environment. This is **model-specific internal verification**, not a first-principles prediction of Standard Model interactions or Einstein field equations. Several scripts contain deliberately chosen dynamical interactions, sampling schemes, and transfer normalisations. Preserve them as separate branches instead of merging incompatible operators by their common dimensions.
