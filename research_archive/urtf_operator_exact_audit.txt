EXACT AUDIT OF THE ARCHIVED URTF OPERATOR

Operator:
T_delta(x) = [x - delta*tanh(x/delta)] * [1 + (pi/phi)*delta^2]

Let A(delta) = 1 + (pi/phi)*delta^2.

Fixed points:
1. x = 0 for every delta > 0.
2. x = +/-x_c(delta), where y=x_c/delta solves
       tanh(y) = [(A-1)/A] y.

Derivative:
T'_delta(x) = A(delta)*tanh^2(x/delta).

Consequences:
- T'_delta(0) = 0 for every positive delta, so x=0 is superstable.
- At +/-x_c, |T'| > 1, so those points are unstable separatrices.
- |x_0| < x_c eventually collapses to zero.
- |x_0| > x_c runs away in magnitude.

Conclusion:
This archived operator does contain an exact collapse/runaway boundary, but the
boundary is in state amplitude x, not at a special value of delta. Its basin
threshold x_c(delta) varies smoothly through both delta_star and delta_classic.
Neither rail is a bifurcation of this operator. A distinct stage-cascade
recurrence, normalization, or boundary condition is still required to derive
the two delta rails.
