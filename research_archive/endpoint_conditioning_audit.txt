URT ENDPOINT-CONDITIONING AUDIT
===============================

Question
--------
Does replacing the frozen Gate-2 source filtration

    M_f = raw_f R_Delta

by terminal/codomain filtration

    M_f = R_Delta raw_f

reproduce the frozen CKM and PMNS matrices without retuning?

Method
------
1. Reconstructed the exact 42-edge centred-icosahedral seed, A5 carrier bases,
   hidden H3/H5 quartets, normalized intertwiners, edge projectors and charge
   frames from the archived seed and Gate-2 solver.
2. Re-solved the classical + CAR-backreacted vacuum at eta_Delta and eta_conf.
3. Validated the reconstruction by reproducing the archived source-filter
   CKM, PMNS, Jarlskog invariants and mass ratios.
4. Changed only the multiplication order from raw_f @ R_Delta to
   R_Delta @ raw_f and repeated the complete calculation.

Verdict
-------
NO. Terminal/codomain placement removes the structural near-identity
obstruction, but it does not reproduce the frozen CKM or PMNS matrices.
It changes under-mixing into severe over-mixing.

Therefore the proposed endpoint-conditioning theorem is not a completed
flavour solution. Matrix-side placement alone is insufficient. A unique,
sector-dependent terminal frame or path-conditioned depth operator would
still have to be derived and tested without target-based branch selection.
