GOLDEN CLOSURE FINAL AUDIT
==========================

1. The published golden correction acts directly on the three standard mixing
   coordinates and one CP phase in each sector: eight real controls total.
2. The diagnostic ledger contains eight real observables.
3. The numerical Jacobian from controls to observables has rank 8. By the
   inverse-function theorem, any sufficiently nearby ledger can be matched by
   choosing those eight controls.
4. Solving exactly from the rounded Platonic baseline gives the residual-derived
   controls:

    19.437014135621343
   -19.640373045757705
    -0.7148318008271523
    -1.2193732777019597
    -6.221355559349670
     8.239502923046901
     0.3587252251010587
   The published phi/count combinations are nearby approximants to these values.
5. The coordinatewise correction is not a covariant Hilbert-space operator.
6. Canonical covariant golden-impedance replacements based on the species charge
   projector and passive Gram matrices were tested at phi, phi^2, Delta phi^3,
   and Delta phi^6 strengths. None reproduced CKM and PMNS simultaneously.

VERDICT
-------
The claimed golden numerical closure is a phenomenological residual fit, not a
first-principles derivation. Phi is present in the exact icosahedral spectrum,
but the current axioms do not specify the noncommuting species-loaded operator
needed to turn that spectrum into the physical flavour frames.
