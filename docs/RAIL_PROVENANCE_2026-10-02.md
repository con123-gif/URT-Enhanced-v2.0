The two Cathedral rails were not introduced as adjustable parameters chosen to reproduce downstream observables.

That chronology matters.

## Bandwidth

Once the two rails are fixed,

```

```

is no longer a selectable number.

It is a consequence of the coexistence of the two rails.

This is why "discovered" is the appropriate project-language distinction:

- the rails were found as structural quantities in the finite construction;
- their meaning and derivational provenance were refined afterward;
- they were not introduced as free knobs for downstream fits.

## Scientific qualification

Discovery inside a mathematical framework does not by itself prove that nature realizes that framework.

The strongest evidence comes when quantities fixed upstream survive blind downstream tests without retuning.

The Cathedral provenance claim is therefore:

> d_cl, d_* and Delta are upstream structural quantities of the framework, not adjustable phenomenological parameters.

The theory-of-nature test is whether the physical identifications derived from them continue to survive independent validation.
