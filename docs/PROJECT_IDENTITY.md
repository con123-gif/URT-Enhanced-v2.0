# Newton’s Cathedral — Project Identity

## Name

**Newton’s Cathedral**

## Description

**A mathematical oddity — working towards a theory of nature.**

Newton’s Cathedral is the overall research programme.

Universal Recursive Tuning (**URT**) is a foundational selection/dynamical principle inside Newton’s Cathedral. It is not the name of the whole project.

## Research position

Newton’s Cathedral is **not being presented as a completed theory of nature**.

The programme is working towards that standard by attempting to derive, from one internally coherent mathematical structure:

- admissible history selection;
- information and entropy geometry;
- finite geometry and quantum structure;
- matter and flavour;
- gauge interactions;
- gravity and spacetime;
- cosmology;
- and ultimately the transition to complex matter.

Every claimed closure must remain separated from its physical identification, continuum completion and empirical validation.
